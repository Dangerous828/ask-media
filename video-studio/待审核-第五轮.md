### 真实项目用的什么写法

```text
<Subject 2> is the stylized Q-version 3D anime penguin girl in <Picture 1>,
featuring premium cinema-grade 3D cartoon CGI rendering, fair porcelain skin,
large dark-grey eyes, soft blush on cheeks, straight black bangs with a bob haircut,
a light-blue paperclip hair accessory on the right bangs, wearing a smooth black
```

- **大量具体材质描述**（soft blush、straight black bangs、padded fleece、silver-metallic collar）
