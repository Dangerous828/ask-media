# 信息接力｜稳定载体穿越多种实体媒介

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one stable carrier and the first concise original message state before the first medium change.
- Advance a related sequence of short messages through at least three media with visibly different material behavior.
- Change camera duty to prove each medium's physics while keeping the carrier or consequence traceable.
- Resolve the final message state in a new terminal medium and hold it without returning to the opening state.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

固定一个可追踪人物或载体，让一组相关但不断推进的短信息依次进入性质明显不同的实体媒介；每次转换都由人物动作或镜头路线触发，并用新的材质物理证明信息链连续，最终在新的终端媒介中完成清楚锁定，而不是回到起点。

# 信息接力｜稳定载体穿越多种实体媒介 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one stable carrier and the first concise readable message state, then advance a related sequence of short messages through at least three materially different physical or graphical media. Each new phrase must progress the informational state rather than repeat arbitrary slogans, every handoff must be motivated by carrier movement, contact or an explicit spatial transition, and each medium must demonstrate distinct physics rather than only a new font. Vary camera duty to prove those physics, preserve the carrier or its consequence, and finish on a clear terminal message in a new medium without returning to the opening state. Source wording, performer design and exact media order are not reusable.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one stable carrier and the first concise original message state before the first medium change.
- Purpose: Defines the identity and informational starting point of the relay.
- Failure if removed: Without a stable carrier and initial message state, later scenes become unrelated typography shots.

### inv-02

- Rule: Advance a related sequence of short messages through at least three media with visibly different material behavior.
- Purpose: Proves a coherent informational progression and cross-medium continuity.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one word without progression, or swapping only color and font, does not form the observed evolving-message relay.

### inv-03

- Rule: Make each handoff causal through carrier movement, contact or a readable spatial boundary.
- Purpose: Connects the media changes into one forward process.
- Failure if removed: Arbitrary montage cuts remove the sense of transfer.

### inv-04

- Rule: Change camera duty to prove each medium's physics while keeping the carrier or consequence traceable.
- Purpose: Makes material differences inspectable without losing continuity.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one frontal beauty frame hides whether the media behave differently.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the final message state in a new terminal medium and hold it without returning to the opening state.
- Purpose: Gives the informational relay an irreversible finish.
- Failure if removed: A loop back to the first treatment or an unheld slogan collapses the forward progression.

| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |
