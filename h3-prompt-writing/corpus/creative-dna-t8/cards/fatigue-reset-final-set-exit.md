2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Lock one observational camera on an adult performer, bounded workspace and unfinished task.
- Prove prior fatigue through posture and breathing before one bounded recovery action.
- Show at least two comparable controlled repetitions and one visibly harder final repetition without unsafe form.
- Release safely, perform one restrained self-check, exit and hold the empty workspace.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 疲劳回场｜短恢复后完成困难末次并离开 — 摘要

## 用途

用固定观察机位先证明角色已疲劳，让一次短暂恢复动作把呼吸和注意力拉回任务；角色随后主动重返同一工作负荷，完成至少两次可比较的控制重复和一次明显更困难的末次，释放负荷后只做克制自我确认并离开，空场保留完成证据。

## 推荐输入格式

`固定观察机位、成年执行者与未完成任务 + 可见疲劳和呼吸 + 1次有边界的短恢复并收起恢复物 + 主动回到同一任务 + 至少2次可比较控制重复和1次更难末次 + 安全释放、自我确认、离场与空工作区停留`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 成年舞台技师坐在航空箱上喘息，前臂压膝；卷盘、地面标记和未亮绿色指示灯同框；她转向同一手动卷盘，双脚踩回标记点，双手握住既定手柄；她释放张力，确认绿色指示灯，小幅满足呼气后离开；空航空箱、卷盘和绿灯稳定停留

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Lock one observational camera on an adult performer, bounded workspace and unfinished task.
- Prove prior fatigue through posture and breathing before one bounded recovery action.
- Show at least two comparable controlled repetitions and one visibly harder final repetition without unsafe form.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变人物、环境、道具、风格、声音与结局表面，禁止只换名词。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。

# 疲劳回场｜短恢复后完成困难末次并离开 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one observational camera on a bounded work area, the adult performer and the unfinished task. Begin after prior effort with visible fatigue in posture and breathing. Allow one short recovery action using a small object, then require the performer to put that object away and voluntarily re-engage the same task. Show at least two directly comparable controlled repetitions, make the final repetition visibly harder without unsafe form, then release the load safely. End with one restrained self-evaluation, a deliberate exit and an empty held workspace. The reusable mechanism is fatigue proof, bounded reset, voluntary return, comparable repetition, difficult last effort, safe release and empty-frame closure—not the source woman, gym, black sportswear, bottle, bench, cable machine, exact exercise, body, autofocus behavior, camera height, timing, creator identity or unsupported model mapping.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one observational camera on an adult performer, bounded workspace and unfinished task.
- Purpose: Creates a fair before-and-after carrier without editorial assistance.
- Failure if removed: Changing views can hide resets or skipped work.

### inv-02

- Rule: Prove prior fatigue through posture and breathing before one bounded recovery action.
- Purpose: Gives the return meaningful effort cost.
- Failure if removed: A fresh performer makes the final task ordinary.

### inv-03

- Rule: Put the recovery object away and visibly re-engage the same task by choice.
- Purpose: Marks the causal boundary between rest and renewed work.
- Failure if removed: Remaining in rest or cutting into action removes agency.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show at least two comparable controlled repetitions and one visibly harder final repetition without unsafe form.
- Purpose: Builds a readable exertion ladder and terminal challenge.
- Failure if removed: Uncountable montage or dangerous strain weakens proof.

### inv-05

- Rule: Release safely, perform one restrained self-check, exit and hold the empty workspace.
- Purpose: Closes effort, evaluation and departure in one observable chain.
- Failure if removed: Camera follow or celebration removes the quiet completion evidence.
