# 材质片头｜多件物证承载职员表到总标题

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Use at least three materially distinct object vignettes, each with one short readable original credit.
- Bind each credit to one visible material-specific motion that never covers the text.
- Return to one earlier material family before the master title and use the motif as the bridge.
- Resolve one large master title and hold it fully readable with no further event.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 材质片头｜多件物证承载职员表到总标题 Template

## Mechanism

Build a short title sequence from at least three materially different object vignettes. Give each vignette one concise original credit and one material-specific motion that frames rather than obscures the text. Repeat one color, geometric or token motif across the vignettes, then return to an established material family and let the recurring motif bridge into one large master title. Hold the final title without further transformation. The reusable mechanism is material-specific credit relay, recurring motif, gathered transition and master-title lockup—not the source ancient setting, names, relief map, red channel, green stones, mirror vessel, fire figure, pyramid forms, green sphere, exact title, typeface, scene order, creator identity or MiniMax H3 branding.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Use at least three materially distinct object vignettes, each with one short readable original credit.
- Purpose: Creates a credit relay rather than a single decorative title.
- Failure if removed: Repeating one surface makes the middle states interchangeable.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind each credit to one visible material-specific motion that never covers the text.
- Purpose: Makes typography part of the physical scene while preserving readability.
- Failure if removed: Generic crossfades or occluded copy remove the tactile proof.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one color, shape or token motif through every vignette.
- Purpose: Provides continuity across incompatible materials.
- Failure if removed: No recurring motif turns the sequence into unrelated product shots.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return to one earlier material family before the master title and use the motif as the bridge.
- Purpose: Gathers prior evidence into a motivated finale.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated final background makes the title feel appended.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve one large master title and hold it fully readable with no further event.
- Purpose: Provides the terminal information state.
- Failure if removed: Continuing transformations prevent title retention.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Use at least three materially distinct object vignettes, each with one short readable original credit.
- Bind each credit to one visible material-specific motion that never covers the text.
- Return to one earlier material family before the master title and use the motif as the bridge.
- Resolve one large master title and hold it fully readable with no further event.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source names, credits, title wording, typeface and exact typographic hierarchy
- the source ancient relief map, red and turquoise channels, pyramid-like blocks and terrain layout
- the source green stone ring, black textile, mirror vessel, clay pot, flame and miniature figure
- the source green sphere, title sculpture, exact material order, camera moves and lighting cues
- the source creator identity, MiniMax H3 claim, platform overlay, watermark and branding

## Transfer Tests

1. A polar research drama uses frost-cracked slate, braided signal wire, a brass compass lens and blue ice channels to carry three original credits; one amber locator bead recurs across all materials and locks into the final carved title.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. A botanical mystery uses pressed leaves, seed mosaics, a glass specimen dome and irrigation-map channels for three credits; one white seed token crosses every vignette and becomes the dot of the final original title.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family

## Model Skeletons

### MiniMax H3

    integrated_multimodal_description: [new subject, environment, ordered actions, camera, light and final state preserving every anchor]
    overall_soundscape: [diegetic ambience, action cues and causal payoff]
    non_diegetic_music: [original music arc or N/A]

### Seedance 2.0

    总时长约[4–15]秒。
    主体定义：[稳定人物、物体与空间关系]
    事件或镜头：[按因果顺序实现全部锚点]
    整体风格与画质：[原创媒介、光影、色彩与声音]
    约束：[连续性、物理逻辑、反复制与结尾状态]

Adapt shot count to the user's concept. Preserve the causal mechanism, not a fixed storyboard.
