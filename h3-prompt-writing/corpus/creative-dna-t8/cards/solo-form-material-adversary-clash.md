Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one subject's complete movement signature and its material trace before an opponent appears.
- Accumulate the same environmental material around the subject before it gains agency.

- After the collision, restore one readable subject state with the signature action or tool still identifiable.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 独演成敌｜动作材质凝成对手后碰撞 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one subject performing a complete, readable form that establishes both a movement signature and a material trace. Let environmental residue from that performance accumulate before it condenses into an independent adversary; the opponent must visibly originate from the established material family. Use one spatial crossing to bring both agents into a shared confrontation, introduce a contrasting second material only at the late peak, and close on a readable subject state after the material collision.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one subject's complete movement signature and its material trace before an opponent appears.
- Purpose: Creates the action vocabulary and origin material.
- Failure if removed: An immediate enemy gives the material no causal history.

### inv-02

- Rule: Accumulate the same environmental material around the subject before it gains agency.
- Purpose: Makes the adversary an outcome of the established field.
- Failure if removed: A random arriving character is only an unrelated antagonist.

### inv-03

- Rule: Condense the material into an independent opponent and bring both agents onto one readable spatial route.
- Purpose: Converts solo proof into a causal confrontation.
- Failure if removed: Abstract weather without an opponent cannot test the movement signature.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reserve a contrasting second material for one late clash peak.
- Purpose: Creates a measurable escalation threshold.
- Failure if removed: Using both materials from the start flattens the hierarchy.

### inv-05

- Rule: After the collision, restore one readable subject state with the signature action or tool still identifiable.
- Purpose: Closes the arc on agency rather than pure spectacle.
- Failure if removed: Ending inside debris leaves the causal winner and retained skill unclear.
