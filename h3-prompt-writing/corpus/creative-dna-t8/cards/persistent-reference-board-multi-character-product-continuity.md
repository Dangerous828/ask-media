# 多人商品一致性｜常驻参考板与角色接力实证

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Register one persistent reference board containing the original product and at least three clearly adult character identities before the first scene change.
- Give each adult at least three stable identity anchors and give the product stable geometry, proportion, color allocation and mark position.
- Insert one macro material or result proof that is causally linked to the same product.
- Reunite all adults before handing off to one clean isolated product lockup and readable hold.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 多人商品一致性｜常驻参考板与角色接力实证 — 摘要

## 用途

先把原创商品、至少三名明确成年角色和各自身份锚点集中写入一条常驻参考板；主画面依次让角色在不同场景完成商品展示、接力与使用动作，参考板始终作为视觉核验基准。每次换人或换景都保留同一包装比例、标志位置和主色，最后让全部角色与商品同框，再切到无歧义包装终局。

## 推荐输入格式

`常驻注册参考板同时包含至少三名明确成年角色和同一原创商品 + 每名角色至少三项身份锚点 + 商品几何/比例/配色/标志位置固定 + 主画面依次完成单人展示、群体运动、一次使用动作与一次材质结果微距 + 每次换人换景都可对照核验 + 全员同框后交给孤立商品终局并停留`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 左侧固定参考栏同时展示三名成年探险厨师和同一 NORTHLEAF 琥珀茶罐，主画面由银辫角色A在储藏室展示茶罐；角色B接过同一茶罐并把茶叶倒入透明玻璃壶，其他两人保留在景深中；完整三人同框交接到象牙台面上的同一 NORTHLEAF 茶罐和三片鲜叶，包装完全稳定并停留

# 多人商品一致性｜常驻参考板与角色接力实证 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original product and at least three clearly adult characters in a persistent registered reference board that remains visible while the main image changes. Give every adult at least three stable identity anchors and give the product stable package geometry, proportion, color allocation and mark position. In the main image, relay attention between solo presentation, coordinated group movement, one visible use action, one material or result macro and a final group payoff; after every scene or character change, the persistent board must allow immediate visual comparison. End by handing the group composition to a clean isolated product lockup. The reusable mechanism is a constant visual audit baseline, multi-character role relay, product continuity across environments, material proof and group-to-pack closure—not the source people, faces, hair, clothing, snack, package text, corridor, garden, food layers, palette, layouts, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Register one persistent reference board containing the original product and at least three clearly adult character identities before the first scene change.
- Purpose: Creates a fixed audit baseline for every later character and product appearance.
- Failure if removed: Without a simultaneous baseline, continuity claims depend on memory and cannot be visually verified.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give each adult at least three stable identity anchors and give the product stable geometry, proportion, color allocation and mark position.
- Purpose: Separates intentional role changes from identity or package drift.
- Failure if removed: A shared wardrobe color alone cannot prove who or what remained consistent.

### inv-03

- Rule: Relay the main image through solo presentation, coordinated group movement and one visible product-use action while keeping the board registered.
- Purpose: Tests identity and package continuity under materially different staging duties.
- Failure if removed: Repeated static portraits do not prove consistency during motion or interaction.

### inv-04

- Rule: Insert one macro material or result proof that is causally linked to the same product.
- Purpose: Explains what the product delivers rather than offering only character spectacle.
- Failure if removed: A decorative macro with no package handoff breaks the commercial evidence chain.

### inv-05

- Rule: Reunite all adults before handing off to one clean isolated product lockup and readable hold.
- Purpose: Closes both the ensemble and product identity promises.
- Failure if removed: Ending on another character insert leaves the advertised object unresolved.

| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

- Register one persistent reference board containing the original product and at least three clearly adult character identities before the first scene change.

   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
