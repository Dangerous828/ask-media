# 移动兑现｜载具跃迁到生活结果

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish the carrier and departure point before accelerating the route.
- Use one visible capability upgrade that changes how the carrier traverses space.
- Present the delivered object or service in tactile close detail at arrival.
- End with a beneficiary visibly using the result.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 移动兑现｜载具跃迁到生活结果 Template

## Mechanism

A mobility proof becomes meaningful when an identified carrier departs, demonstrates one unmistakable capability upgrade, crosses a legible route, arrives at a specific destination, and hands the sequence to a human-scale benefit. The beneficiary action must visibly consume or use the delivered result rather than merely posing beside it.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish the carrier and departure point before accelerating the route.
- Purpose: Gives the journey a concrete origin.
- Failure if removed: Opening mid-flight removes the state change and route promise.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible capability upgrade that changes how the carrier traverses space.
- Purpose: Creates a technical proof rather than ordinary travel.
- Failure if removed: Decorative light with no route effect is not an upgrade.

### inv-03

- Rule: Preserve a readable destination transition instead of random travel montage.
- Purpose: Connects performance to arrival.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated locations make the journey untraceable.

### inv-04

- Rule: Present the delivered object or service in tactile close detail at arrival.
- Purpose: Turns distance into a concrete result.
- Failure if removed: Skipping arrival detail leaves the benefit abstract.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with a beneficiary visibly using the result.
- Purpose: Completes the technology-to-life causal chain.
- Failure if removed: A product beauty shot does not prove human benefit.

| Slot | Constraint |
