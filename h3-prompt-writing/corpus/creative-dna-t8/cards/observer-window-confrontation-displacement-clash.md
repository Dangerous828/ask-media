2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one observer boundary, two adult participants, the collision lane and a consequence surface before escalation.
- Carry the first gap closure into one visible environmental damage result.

- Let the final collision transform or obscure the same observer boundary.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 见证窗不切换｜对峙、越界位移与能量碰撞留在同一场 Template

## Mechanism

Choose one original observer boundary such as a window, booth or balcony and use it to establish two clearly adult participants, a collision lane and at least one environmental consequence surface. Keep that boundary recoverable through the sequence. After a readable confrontation baseline, cause one participant to cross the gap and displace the other into the consequence surface; then return both participants to the same arena with the damage retained. Build two opposed charge states across the original axis and let the final collision expand into or obscure the observer boundary. The reusable mechanism is bounded witness geography, displacement-to-damage causality, arena return and opposed collision—not the source school, students, copyrighted characters, hairstyles, costumes, crowd, powers, wall, camera position, style, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one observer boundary, two adult participants, the collision lane and a consequence surface before escalation.
- Purpose: Gives every impossible action a measurable shared arena.
- Failure if removed: Without a baseline, displacement and scale cannot be verified.

### inv-02

- Rule: Carry the first gap closure into one visible environmental damage result.
- Purpose: Makes the attack legible through a destination and residue.
- Failure if removed: A fast blur without a consequence reads as a missing shot.

### inv-03

- Rule: Return both participants to the original arena while retaining the damage state.
- Purpose: Preserves event continuity and cumulative consequence.
- Failure if removed: Resetting the wall or changing location breaks the causal chain.

### inv-04

- Rule: Build two distinct opposed charge states across the established collision axis.
- Purpose: Prepares a readable terminal interaction rather than arbitrary effects.
- Failure if removed: Unoriented energy makes the final impact spatially ambiguous.

### inv-05

- Rule: Let the final collision transform or obscure the same observer boundary.
- Purpose: Closes participant action, environment and witness perspective together.
- Failure if removed: A detached explosion insert abandons the promised viewpoint.

| Slot | Constraint |

| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- the source vertical observer composition, camera motion, timing, choreography and photoreal treatment
- the source creator identity, GPT Image 2 and Seedance 2.5 claims, watermark, overlay and branding

A first shove dents a marked foam barrier, the yard resets with the dent retained, and blue versus amber field states collide into a harmless vapor bloom against the window.

   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
