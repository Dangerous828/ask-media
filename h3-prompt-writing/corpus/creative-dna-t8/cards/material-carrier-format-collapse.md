# 材质引路｜空间生长到版式定格

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Introduce one small material guide on the same optical axis as a stable subject.
- Keep one continuous material family responsible for camera guidance, color accumulation and spatial growth.
- Expand the same material into an environment that still contains the original subject and prior palette.
- Use one late camera rise to resolve the dimensional scene into a held graphic sheet that preserves subject and material history.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 材质引路｜空间生长到版式定格 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with one small material guide aligned to a stable subject. Keep that same material visually continuous as it pulls the camera, accumulates color and depth, becomes one readable physical structure, and expands into an inhabitable environment. Reserve one final camera rise that compresses the dimensional world into a held graphic sheet while preserving the subject and material history.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Introduce one small material guide on the same optical axis as a stable subject.
- Purpose: Creates a precise origin for the transformation.
- Failure if removed: Without a small origin and stable subject, the sequence starts as an ungrounded effects montage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one continuous material family responsible for camera guidance, color accumulation and spatial growth.
- Purpose: Makes every phase feel causally related.
- Failure if removed: Changing material rules between phases breaks the transformation chain.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let the material form one readable, depth-bearing structure inside the scene and hold it briefly.
- Purpose: Provides a recognition checkpoint before scale expansion.
- Failure if removed: Flat overlay copy or unreadable decoration cannot prove physical integration.

### inv-04

- Rule: Expand the same material into an environment that still contains the original subject and prior palette.
- Purpose: Turns a local effect into a world-scale payoff without losing identity.
- Failure if removed: A disconnected new room would read as a scene swap.

### inv-05

- Rule: Use one late camera rise to resolve the dimensional scene into a held graphic sheet that preserves subject and material history.
- Purpose: Closes spatial spectacle with an inspectable format change.
- Failure if removed: An arbitrary end card would not prove continuity with the preceding world.
