# 未来来信｜私密警告被外部世界兑现

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one private proof record with a visibly impossible future origin.
- Bind the record to a future version or unmistakable continuation of the present subject.
- First reveal a familiar environment in a clearly altered future state.
- Introduce one larger external anomaly only after the environment confirms the time shift, and end unresolved.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 未来来信｜私密警告被外部世界兑现 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with a private record that is visibly dated or sourced from a future version of the subject. Keep its message bounded inside the proof device, then make the present subject move to one stable external aperture for verification. Reveal a familiar environment in an impossible future state, and only after that first confirmation introduce a much larger external anomaly that reframes the warning as an immediate unresolved threat.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one private proof record with a visibly impossible future origin.
- Purpose: Creates a falsifiable information anomaly.
- Failure if removed: A vague mysterious message lacks temporal proof.

### inv-02

- Rule: Bind the record to a future version or unmistakable continuation of the present subject.
- Purpose: Makes verification personally motivated.
- Failure if removed: An unrelated messenger weakens the identity bridge.

### inv-03

- Rule: Move the present subject from the private record to one stable external aperture.
- Purpose: Turns information into an observable test.
- Failure if removed: A cut directly to spectacle removes the verification action.

### inv-04

- Rule: First reveal a familiar environment in a clearly altered future state.
- Purpose: Provides a local confirmation before escalation.
- Failure if removed: Skipping local confirmation makes the warning ungrounded.

### inv-05

- Rule: Introduce one larger external anomaly only after the environment confirms the time shift, and end unresolved.
- Purpose: Escalates evidence into threat without overexplaining it.
- Failure if removed: An early giant reveal consumes the evidence ladder.
