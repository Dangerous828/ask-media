# 被拍到的一天｜观察者瑕疵伴随路线，人物最终发现镜头

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Lock one clearly adult original subject with at least three clothing, object or gait anchors across four ordinary chapters.
- Use only bounded observer imperfections and always recover the subject and route.
- Do not let the subject directly acknowledge the camera before the final chapter.
- End with one clear eye-line change and short approach toward the lens.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 被拍到的一天｜观察者瑕疵伴随路线，人物最终发现镜头 — 摘要

## 用途

同一明确成年人沿一条普通日常路线完成至少四件小事，摄影保持可控的迟跟、遮挡、构图偏差与呼吸式晃动，让观察者存在但不抢叙事；人物早期不看镜头，终局才通过一次清楚视线与靠近动作发现仍在拍摄。

## 推荐输入格式

`一名明确成年原创主体以至少三项服装、携带物或步态锚点贯穿至少四个普通路线章节 + 使用迟跟、短遮挡、克制对焦恢复和呼吸晃动等受控观察瑕疵且总能找回主体 + 每章至少携带一个物件、方向或完成动作进入下一章 + 终章前人物不得直接承认镜头 + 结尾以清楚眼线变化和短距离靠近证明人物发现记录者`

# 被拍到的一天｜观察者瑕疵伴随路线，人物最终发现镜头 Template

## Mechanism

Follow one original clearly adult subject through at least four ordinary route chapters with stable clothing, carried-object or gait anchors. Maintain deliberate but bounded observer imperfections—slightly late pans, short foreground occlusions, modest focus recovery and breathing-scale sway—while always returning to the subject and route. The subject must avoid direct camera acknowledgment until the final chapter. End with one unmistakable eye-line change and short approach that reveals awareness of the recorder. The reusable mechanism is ordinary route continuity, controlled observer imperfection, delayed acknowledgment and camera-discovery closure—not the source man, apartment, market, fruit, cat, rooftop, exact framing, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original subject with at least three clothing, object or gait anchors across four ordinary chapters.
- Purpose: Maintains identity through everyday cuts.
- Failure if removed: Unanchored chapter changes suggest different people or days.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use only bounded observer imperfections and always recover the subject and route.
- Purpose: Creates authentic home-video presence without losing readability.
- Failure if removed: Continuous random shake or focus loss becomes unusable footage.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one object, direction or completed action from each chapter into the next.
- Purpose: Links small events into a day.
- Failure if removed: Independent activities form a loose montage.

### inv-04

- Rule: Do not let the subject directly acknowledge the camera before the final chapter.
- Purpose: Preserves the observer relationship and delayed reveal.
- Failure if removed: Early eye contact removes the ending's meaning.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with one clear eye-line change and short approach toward the lens.
- Purpose: Makes camera discovery observable.
- Failure if removed: A generic wave or cut cannot prove the subject noticed recording.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |
