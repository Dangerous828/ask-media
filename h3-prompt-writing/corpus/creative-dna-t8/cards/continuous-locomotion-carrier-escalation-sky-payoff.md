Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Lock one clearly adult original identity and one forward movement direction before the first handoff.
- Trigger every carrier change at a visible physical threshold such as an edge, wake, contact or launch.
- Escalate surface, carrier scale or height monotonically across at least four states.
- End with the same adult silhouette released at the highest scale or altitude.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

## 用途

同一明确成年主体沿一个前进方向连续移动，每次只在速度、表面或高度达到阈值后，把运动完整交给下一种原创载具或伙伴；身份、方向和动量不重置。由地面、浪面、生物背部逐级升高，最后用高空释放兑现升级。

## 推荐输入格式

`同一明确成年原创主体、至少三项身份锚点与单一前进方向先建立 + 至少四种移动状态都在可见边缘、接触、尾迹或发射阈值发生交接 + 每次至少继承速度、姿态或尾迹之一 + 接触表面、载体尺度或高度单调升级 + 终局同一成年人在最高层级完成短促空中释放`

# 连续载具升级｜滑行、冲浪、生物接力到高空释放 Template

## Mechanism

Keep one original clearly adult protagonist moving continuously along one established direction. Use at least four locomotion states whose carrier, contact surface or scale changes, but require every change to occur at a visible physical threshold and inherit the previous velocity, body posture or wake. Escalate monotonically from ground contact to water surface, larger living or mechanical support, and finally increased height. Never reset the adult identity or movement axis between states. End with a brief elevated release that still preserves the same silhouette. The reusable mechanism is continuous motion inheritance, threshold-based carrier handoff, monotonic scale-and-height escalation and airborne payoff—not the source woman, island styling, skateboard, surfboard, animals, ocean, choreography, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original identity and one forward movement direction before the first handoff.
- Purpose: Creates continuity across carrier changes.
- Failure if removed: Changing identity or direction makes separate clips rather than one route.

### inv-02

- Rule: Trigger every carrier change at a visible physical threshold such as an edge, wake, contact or launch.
- Purpose: Gives each transition a causal bridge.
- Failure if removed: Open morphs or arbitrary cuts break motion inheritance.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry at least one velocity, posture or wake cue through every transition.
- Purpose: Proves that movement continues rather than restarts.
- Failure if removed: A stationary incoming carrier resets momentum.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate surface, carrier scale or height monotonically across at least four states.
- Purpose: Builds a readable progression.
- Failure if removed: Randomly shrinking or returning to earlier states weakens the payoff.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the same adult silhouette released at the highest scale or altitude.
- Purpose: Completes the ladder while preserving identity.
- Failure if removed: An anonymous landscape or different rider loses the route.
