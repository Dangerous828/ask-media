# 语义字效｜短语物理逐级升级到整句锁定

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Begin with one original sentence divided into a fixed semantic order; reveal the first phrase from a minimal baseline.
- Give every new phrase one readable typographic state and one physical behavior tied to its semantic role.
- Escalate at least two of scale, contrast, spatial depth or motion energy in a controlled sequence.
- Stop the escalation and reassemble the complete sentence in a stable readable final hold.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 语义字效｜短语物理逐级升级到整句锁定 Template

## Mechanism

Split one original sentence into ordered semantic phrases. Give each phrase one legible typographic state and one physical operation that expresses its meaning, while preserving a visible carrier from the previous phase. Increase contrast, spatial scale and motion energy in controlled steps, then reverse the priority at the end: stop all movement, restore the full sentence and hold it long enough to read. The reusable mechanism is semantic phrase-to-physics escalation followed by complete-language lockup, not the source quotation, fonts, palette or exact effects.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Begin with one original sentence divided into a fixed semantic order; reveal the first phrase from a minimal baseline.
- Purpose: Creates a language-first causal spine.
- Failure if removed: A decorative opening without ordered text gives later effects nothing to develop.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give every new phrase one readable typographic state and one physical behavior tied to its semantic role.
- Purpose: Makes meaning drive motion instead of decorating it.
- Failure if removed: Generic transitions or illegible words sever the phrase-to-effect relationship.

### inv-03

- Rule: Carry one visible element, material or motion consequence from each phase into the next.
- Purpose: Maintains one continuous argument across changing designs.
- Failure if removed: Independent title cards become an unrelated typography reel.

### inv-04

- Rule: Escalate at least two of scale, contrast, spatial depth or motion energy in a controlled sequence.
- Purpose: Builds retention and makes the terminal maximum earned.
- Failure if removed: Equal-intensity phases flatten the arc; simultaneous excess becomes chaotic.

### inv-05

- Rule: Stop the escalation and reassemble the complete sentence in a stable readable final hold.
- Purpose: Converts spectacle back into comprehension.
- Failure if removed: Ending on fragments or one giant word leaves the language unresolved.
