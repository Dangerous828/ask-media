````text
# 目标先现的仪式行进｜前景队列与地标尺度同步放大

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Show one monumental destination, one readable route and at least three clearly adult travelers or carriers together in the opening.
- Preserve one objective direction and one stable destination identity throughout.
- Combine forward travel with a restrained push and slight elevation change so destination scale layers increase monotonically.
- Hold a destination-dominant final view while retaining at least two traveler or route anchors.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.
````

### summary.md

````text
# 目标先现的仪式行进｜前景队列与地标尺度同步放大 — 摘要

## 用途

开场就完整展示宏大目的地、可读路线和一组前景行进者，不靠隐藏或反转制造看点。全片保持同一朝向，让行进者沿路线持续缩短与地标的距离，同时用重复旗帜、服装或载体建立队列节奏；摄影机只做克制推进和轻微升高，结尾让人物仍在场但目的地占据更大画面比例，以尺度增长兑现庄严感。

## 推荐输入格式

`完整宏大目的地、可读接近路线与至少三名明确成年旅行者或载体在开场同现 + 全程保持单一目标方向和稳定目的地身份 + 旗帜、灯光、服装或载体等群体锚点重复形成仪式节奏 + 行进与克制前推、轻微升高共同让地标尺度层单调增加 + 终局目的地主导但仍保留至少两个旅行者或路线锚点`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 发光冰晶研究站位于冻海对岸，雪路和三名成年研究员驾驶的履带雪橇在前景；研究站下层停靠墙、冰桥和上层温室梯田依次更清楚，雪橇逐渐变小；研究站、冰桥和梯田占据主要画面，下面仍有两辆雪橇、雪路和橙灯
````

````text
# 目标先现的仪式行进｜前景队列与地标尺度同步放大 Template

## Mechanism

Reveal one original monumental destination in full at the opening together with a clearly readable approach route and at least three clearly adult travelers or carriers in the foreground. Preserve one objective direction and one stable destination identity for the entire shot. Use repeated group anchors such as flags, garments, lights or carrier forms to turn ordinary forward movement into a processional rhythm. Combine traveler motion with one restrained camera push and slight elevation change so the destination gains architectural or environmental scale layers monotonically while foreground figures reduce. End on a held destination-dominant wide that retains at least two small traveler or route anchors, proving continued relationship rather than replacing the journey with a disconnected postcard. The reusable mechanism is destination-first promise, processional repetition, continuous approach, monotonic landmark scale gain and traveler-retaining terminal grandeur—not the source Camelot premise, knights, horses, heraldry, castle, valley, flags, route, sunset palette, exact composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Show one monumental destination, one readable route and at least three clearly adult travelers or carriers together in the opening.
- Purpose: Creates a destination-first promise with measurable scale and direction.
- Failure if removed: Hiding the destination changes the mechanism into a reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Preserve one objective direction and one stable destination identity throughout.
- Purpose: Makes all motion contribute to the same approach.
- Failure if removed: Direction reversals or landmark substitution break journey continuity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one repeated group anchor to create processional rhythm across the moving foreground.
- Purpose: Turns generic travel into a collective ceremonial action.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated travelers read as traffic rather than a procession.

### inv-04

- Rule: Combine forward travel with a restrained push and slight elevation change so destination scale layers increase monotonically.
- Purpose: Builds grandeur without cuts, transformations or surprise events.
- Failure if removed: Aggressive orbiting or scale regression makes the geography unstable.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold a destination-dominant final view while retaining at least two traveler or route anchors.
- Purpose: Proves the journey relationship survives the scale payoff.
- Failure if removed: Removing every traveler turns the ending into a disconnected landscape image.
````
