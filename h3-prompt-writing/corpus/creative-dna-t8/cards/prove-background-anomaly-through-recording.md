1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Reveal the anomaly to the viewer before the subjects notice it.
- Keep foreground behavior ordinary during the first anomaly state.

- Delay the human reaction until after the proof changes.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

## 用途

先让异常出现在背景而人物继续日常活动，再用记录设备把异常隔离成证据；异常改变或离开记录画面后，人物才出现延迟反应。

## 推荐输入格式

`背景异常先出现 + 前景保持日常 + 记录设备取证 + 证据场变化 + 延迟反应`

# 背景异常｜先被观众发现再由记录设备证实 Template

## Mechanism

Place an anomaly in the background before foreground subjects notice it, preserve ordinary foreground behavior, use a recording or inspection device to isolate the anomaly as evidence, let the anomaly visibly disrupt or leave that evidence field, then delay the subjects' reaction until after the proof changes.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Reveal the anomaly to the viewer before the subjects notice it.
- Purpose: Creates dramatic information asymmetry.
- Failure if removed: Simultaneous discovery removes suspense.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep foreground behavior ordinary during the first anomaly state.
- Purpose: Makes the background disruption legible.
- Failure if removed: Immediate panic spends the escalation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use one device or inspection frame to isolate the anomaly.
- Purpose: Turns suspicion into observable evidence.
- Failure if removed: A random close-up does not prove discovery.

### inv-04

- Rule: Change the anomaly inside or against the evidence field.
- Purpose: Makes the proof active rather than static.
- Failure if removed: An unchanged recording adds no causal turn.

### inv-05

- Rule: Delay the human reaction until after the proof changes.
- Purpose: Completes the viewer-subject information handoff.
- Failure if removed: Early reaction breaks the asymmetry.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair whose role is clear without source identity. |
| setting | A materially new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific location. |
| causal_carrier | One object, route, mark or device that visibly carries the causal chain. |
| state_change | Five ordered states with observable handoffs, not a decorative montage. |
| camera_grammar | A new proof-oriented information order that does not copy source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A held final consequence that resolves or recontextualizes the setup. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of story-world cues supporting each visible change. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Reveal the anomaly to the viewer before the subjects notice it.
- Keep foreground behavior ordinary during the first anomaly state.
- Change the anomaly inside or against the evidence field.
- Delay the human reaction until after the proof changes.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source anime women and neon alley
- purple ghost silhouette and smartphone screen
- the exact phone-recording disappearance storyboard
- source character designs and palette
- source phone 00:00 recording UI
- source red record button

## Transfer Tests

1. A single greenhouse engineer calibrates valves while a six-branch frost glyph appears on a distant pressure window; a fixed mechanical observation mirror isolates the glyph, which migrates along a copper pipe before the engineer finally turns.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
2. An autonomous archive camera inventories folded fabric while a geometric shadow appears behind one shelf; a fixed ultraviolet light table isolates its lattice, the pattern transfers onto one cloth strip, and only then does the camera pivot toward the now-normal shelf.
   - Preserved: inv-01, inv-02, inv-03, inv-04, inv-05
   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, causal_carrier, state_change, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family

## Model Skeletons

    overall_soundscape: [diegetic ambience, action cues and causal payoff]

### Seedance 2.0

    总时长约[4–15]秒。
    主体定义：[稳定人物、物体与空间关系]
    事件或镜头：[按因果顺序实现全部锚点]
    整体风格与画质：[原创媒介、光影、色彩与声音]
    约束：[连续性、物理逻辑、反复制与结尾状态]

Adapt shot count to the user's concept. Preserve the causal mechanism, not a fixed storyboard.
