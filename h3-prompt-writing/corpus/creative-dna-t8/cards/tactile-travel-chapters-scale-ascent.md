# 触觉旅行章法｜近物接触到高位全景递进

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Keep one traveler identity, wardrobe anchor and forward route direction across all chapters.
- Give every location one brief physical contact with a local material or object.
- Use one consistent archival-medium rule across all chapters.
- Resolve the route through a climb and stable high-position hold.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 触觉旅行章法｜近物接触到高位全景递进 Template

## Mechanism

Bind one original traveler through at least four geographically distinct chapters using stable identity, wardrobe and forward direction. In each chapter, require one brief hand, body or tool contact with a location-specific material before moving on. Order the chapters so spatial scale expands from constrained passage and near object, through a dense social field and moving background geography, to a final climb and elevated overview. Apply one consistent archival medium system across all locations. The reusable mechanism is tactile chapter binding, scale expansion and ascent payoff—not the source traveler, Istanbul, blue blouse, cream skirt, leather bag, wooden door, oranges, spice market, ferry, flower, stone stairs, skyline, exact film stock, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Keep one traveler identity, wardrobe anchor and forward route direction across all chapters.
- Purpose: Makes separate locations read as one continuous memory journey.
- Failure if removed: Identity or direction resets turn the piece into unrelated postcards.

### inv-02

- Rule: Give every location one brief physical contact with a local material or object.
- Purpose: Creates embodied evidence and chapter-specific memory anchors.
- Failure if removed: Pure sightseeing produces generic scenic montage.

### inv-03

- Rule: Expand spatial scale from constrained passage through social and geographic breadth to an elevated overview.
- Purpose: Builds a readable progression rather than equal-weight clips.
- Failure if removed: Random scale order removes the final destination payoff.

### inv-04

- Rule: Use one consistent archival-medium rule across all chapters.
- Purpose: Binds lighting and location changes into one remembered document.
- Failure if removed: Changing defects or render media per cut fragments the archive.

### inv-05

- Rule: Resolve the route through a climb and stable high-position hold.
- Purpose: Turns movement into a destination with visible geographic understanding.
- Failure if removed: Ending mid-route or on another close object leaves no culmination.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Keep one traveler identity, wardrobe anchor and forward route direction across all chapters.
- Give every location one brief physical contact with a local material or object.
- Use one consistent archival-medium rule across all chapters.
- Resolve the route through a climb and stable high-position hold.

- the source woman's identity, hair, blue blouse, cream skirt, leather shoulder bag and exact gestures

- the source visible people, signage, boats, urban layout and location-specific architecture
- the source creator identity, Seedance 2.5 claim, platform overlay, watermark and branding
