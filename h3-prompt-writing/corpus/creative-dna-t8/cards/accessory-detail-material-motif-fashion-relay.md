# 配饰材质接力｜细节证据、全身回连与原创品牌收束

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Lock one clearly adult original model and one original material motif before product close-ups.
- Use at least four different accessory classes and one tactile structural proof per class.
- Reconnect detail modules to the same adult's full-body posture and gait.
- Finish with a completely original short brand name and no source product silhouette or mark.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 配饰材质接力｜细节证据、全身回连与原创品牌收束 — 摘要

## 用途

同一明确成年模特与一种原创材质高光贯穿四类配饰证据；每章先用一次触觉动作证明结构，再让同一高光或纹理从当前物件匹配到下一物件。终局回到全身步态和所有配饰关系，并只显示全新原创名称，彻底移除来源品牌。

## 推荐输入格式

`一名明确成年原创模特、至少三项身份锚点和一种原创材质母题先建立 + 至少四类不同配饰且每章先完成一次触觉或结构证明 + 同一高光、纹理、边缘或声音逐件匹配接力 + 细节模块最终回连同一成年人的完整姿态与步态 + 只以完全原创短品牌收束且不得保留来源标志与产品轮廓`

# 配饰材质接力｜细节证据、全身回连与原创品牌收束 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one original clearly adult model with at least three identity anchors and one original material motif. Move through at least four different accessory classes. In each module, show one tactile operation or structural proof before allowing the same highlight, texture, edge geometry or sound to match into the next object. After the detail ladder, reconnect all objects to the same adult's full-body posture and gait. End on one completely original short brand name with no source marks or product silhouettes. The reusable mechanism is tactile accessory evidence, material-motif relay, scale expansion, full-body reconnection and original identity closure—not the source model, luxury brand, monogram, product designs, white set, exact shots, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original model and one original material motif before product close-ups.
- Purpose: Provides human and visual continuity.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated hands and products feel like a catalog collage.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use at least four different accessory classes and one tactile structural proof per class.
- Purpose: Demonstrates breadth and function.
- Failure if removed: Beauty-only close-ups lack evidence of object behavior.

### inv-03

- Rule: Match the same highlight, texture, edge or sound from each item into the next.
- Purpose: Creates a causal visual relay.
- Failure if removed: Hard cuts between unrelated objects lose campaign coherence.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reconnect detail modules to the same adult's full-body posture and gait.
- Purpose: Shows how objects form one look.
- Failure if removed: Ending on isolated products loses the wearer.

### inv-05

- Rule: Finish with a completely original short brand name and no source product silhouette or mark.
- Purpose: Creates distributable identity closure.
- Failure if removed: Source branding or lookalike shapes violate anti-copy boundaries.
