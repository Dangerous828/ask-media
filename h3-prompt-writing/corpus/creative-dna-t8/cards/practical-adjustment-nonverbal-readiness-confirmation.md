# 实用整理确认关系｜接触、退开与双向微反应

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish two clearly adult original roles, one visible irregularity and one stable shared camera axis.
- Use exactly one continuous, functionally clear two-hand adjustment.
- Give both adults different restrained microreactions only after contact ends.
- Return to a shared composition where the finished item and stable postures remain visible.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 实用整理确认关系｜接触、退开与双向微反应 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two original clearly adult roles in one quiet space, one visible garment or equipment irregularity and one stable shared camera axis. Let the helper perform exactly one continuous, functionally legible adjustment with both hands while the receiving adult remains responsive but restrained. Show the adjusted item reaching a measurable finished state before the helper pauses and withdraws. Give each adult one distinct microreaction after contact ends, without relying on unverified dialogue semantics, then return to a shared composition where the finished item and both stable postures prove readiness. The reusable mechanism is practical contact, continuous adjustment, completion pause, separation, reciprocal microreaction and nonverbal readiness confirmation—not the source named characters, faces, hair, uniforms, emblems, neckwear color, franchise, military setting, exact expressions, dialogue, composition, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult original roles, one visible irregularity and one stable shared camera axis.
- Purpose: Makes the practical need and relationship geometry legible before contact.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible issue, touching can read as arbitrary or purely romantic.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use exactly one continuous, functionally clear two-hand adjustment.
- Purpose: Concentrates attention on a verifiable act of care and preparation.
- Failure if removed: Multiple unrelated touches dilute the causal action.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show a measurable finished state before the helper pauses and withdraws.
- Purpose: Proves the task succeeded and creates a transition from action to emotion.
- Failure if removed: Withdrawing before completion leaves both readiness and motivation unresolved.

### inv-04

- Rule: Give both adults different restrained microreactions only after contact ends.
- Purpose: Lets reciprocity emerge from the completed action rather than overt exposition.
- Failure if removed: Large simultaneous reactions overpower the quiet relational mechanism.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to a shared composition where the finished item and stable postures remain visible.
- Purpose: Closes practical and relational evidence together.
- Failure if removed: Ending on an isolated face can hide whether the adjustment persisted.
