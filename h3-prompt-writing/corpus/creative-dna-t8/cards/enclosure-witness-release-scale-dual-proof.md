# 见证释放｜封闭近景—外部尺度—双视图证明

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one close witness inside a bounded enclosure and one partially visible external phenomenon through a fixed opening.
- Use one visible directional action by the witness to transfer attention toward the external phenomenon.
- Give the phenomenon one unobstructed environment-scale view without the witness dominating it.
- End with a simultaneous dual proof of witness response and environmental phenomenon using a new composite geometry.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 见证释放｜封闭近景—外部尺度—双视图证明 Template

## Mechanism

Begin with a close witness-owned view inside a bounded enclosure while an external phenomenon is partially visible through a fixed opening. Use one visible directional action to motivate a camera release from the enclosure, then give the phenomenon an independent wide-scale proof. Finish with a simultaneous dual-view device that keeps witness response and environmental magnitude readable together; the dual proof may use an inset, reflection or monitor and must not copy the source's exact split layout.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one close witness inside a bounded enclosure and one partially visible external phenomenon through a fixed opening.
- Purpose: Creates an immediate scale question with a stable near-far relation.
- Failure if removed: An open wide beginning removes the witness-to-scale reveal.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible directional action by the witness to transfer attention toward the external phenomenon.
- Purpose: Motivates the coming camera release.
- Failure if removed: An unmotivated cut outside separates reaction from what is being witnessed.

### inv-03

- Rule: Release the viewpoint from the enclosure through one readable physical boundary or carrier handoff.
- Purpose: Turns subjective evidence into independently inspectable space.
- Failure if removed: Teleporting to a wide shot loses the experiential transition.

### inv-04

- Rule: Give the phenomenon one unobstructed environment-scale view without the witness dominating it.
- Purpose: Proves magnitude beyond the close reaction frame.
- Failure if removed: Remaining in selfie scale never validates the phenomenon's spatial extent.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with a simultaneous dual proof of witness response and environmental phenomenon using a new composite geometry.
- Purpose: Keeps human-scale and environment-scale evidence available together.
- Failure if removed: Alternating them again weakens the final comparison and closure.
