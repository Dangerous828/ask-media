Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one clearly adult original person, one small nonhuman companion and measurable environment scale.
- Use one visible contact with a declared medium as the sole transformation trigger.
- Make environment reaction scale with body growth while preserving the adult's position and geography.
- End with the same adult and transformed companion in one readable frame that proves final scale.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

## Mechanism

Establish one clearly adult original person, one small nonhuman companion and measurable environment scale. Use one visible contact with a declared medium as the sole transformation trigger. Keep recognizable companion features and displaced medium continuously visible during growth. Make environment reaction scale with body growth while preserving the adult's position and geography. End with the same adult and transformed companion in one readable frame that proves final scale. The reusable mechanism is the ordered baseline, trigger, continuity, controlled escalation and terminal proof—not the source identities, brands, wording, locations, props, exact styling, choreography, camera path, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original person, one small nonhuman companion and measurable environment scale.
- Purpose: Makes the baseline measurable before the mechanism changes it.
- Failure if removed: Without the baseline, later change has no trustworthy reference state.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one visible contact with a declared medium as the sole transformation trigger.
- Purpose: Binds the first visible change to a specific cause rather than decoration.
- Failure if removed: Without a visible trigger, the transition reads as an arbitrary edit.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep recognizable companion features and displaced medium continuously visible during growth.
- Purpose: Preserves identity, material or spatial continuity through the middle transition.
- Failure if removed: Without continuity, the sequence becomes unrelated montage or identity drift.

### inv-04

- Rule: Make environment reaction scale with body growth while preserving the adult's position and geography.
- Purpose: Raises intensity or information while retaining the same readable grammar.
- Failure if removed: Without controlled escalation, the middle either plateaus or turns into noise.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the same adult and transformed companion in one readable frame that proves final scale.
- Purpose: Provides a terminal proof that resolves the mechanism instead of merely stopping.
- Failure if removed: Without terminal proof, the promised result or reversal remains unverified.
