# 事故悬停｜主动取样后独自离场

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish an ordinary routine and the routes of at least two participants before the accident.
- Use one accident to distribute several independently readable objects across depth.
- Have the mobile subject selectively remove at least two separated objects without releasing the rest.
- End with the subject leaving while a wider view still proves the accident remains suspended.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 事故悬停｜主动取样后独自离场 Template

## Mechanism

Establish an ordinary shared routine, interrupt it with one physical accident that distributes several readable objects, and suspend the accident at maximum disorder. Let exactly one subject retain normal agency, deliberately sample at least two separated pieces of the frozen event, then leave while the wider tableau remains visibly locked. The persistent world state, not a later restart, is the payoff.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish an ordinary routine and the routes of at least two participants before the accident.
- Purpose: Makes the interruption causally readable.
- Failure if removed: Opening on suspended debris turns the event into unexplained decoration.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one accident to distribute several independently readable objects across depth.
- Purpose: Creates a testable stopped-world field.
- Failure if removed: One floating prop cannot prove a shared physical state.

### inv-03

- Rule: At peak disorder, keep all participants and objects fixed except one clearly mobile subject.
- Purpose: Gives the anomaly an unambiguous agency rule.
- Failure if removed: If several people move, the freeze becomes an editing ambiguity.

### inv-04

- Rule: Have the mobile subject selectively remove at least two separated objects without releasing the rest.
- Purpose: Proves deliberate interaction inside the fixed state.
- Failure if removed: A passive walk-through never tests the suspension.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with the subject leaving while a wider view still proves the accident remains suspended.
- Purpose: Turns persistence into the final consequence.
- Failure if removed: A global restart would replace the mechanism with a generic time-freeze release.

| Slot | Constraint |
