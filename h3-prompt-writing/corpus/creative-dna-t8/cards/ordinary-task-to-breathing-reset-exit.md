# 日常任务到呼吸复位｜前景过渡、静坐微动与继续离场

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Lock one clearly adult original subject and complete one ordinary tactile task before rest.
- Use one natural foreground or architectural edge to transition into a distinct rest geometry.
- Restrict camera movement during the breath to one slow push or hold.
- After rest, resume the same adult's movement and cross a visible exit threshold.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 日常任务到呼吸复位｜前景过渡、静坐微动与继续离场 — 摘要

## 用途

同一明确成年人先完成一个普通触觉任务，再由前景物或建筑边界把动作段过渡到静坐段；人物只做一次慢呼吸、微笑与细微头发衣料变化，摄影克制推进。复位完成后，人物沿既定方向重新起身离场，证明静默不是终点。

## 推荐输入格式

`一名明确成年原创主体以至少三项身份锚点先完成一个普通触觉任务 + 用一个真实前景物、门框或建筑边缘过渡到独立休息地理 + 准确展示一次慢呼吸和至少两项头发、服装、植物或光线微响应 + 呼吸期间摄影只做克制慢推或静止 + 同一成年人复位后沿既定方向穿过可见出口`

# 日常任务到呼吸复位｜前景过渡、静坐微动与继续离场 Template

## Mechanism

Lock one original clearly adult subject with at least three identity anchors. Begin with one ordinary tactile task and show its completion. Use one natural foreground object, doorway or architectural edge to transition into a separate rest geometry. Hold long enough to show exactly one slow breath and at least two micro-responses in hair, clothing, plants or light while the camera performs only a restrained push. After the reset, let the same adult resume movement along an established direction and cross one visible exit threshold. The reusable mechanism is task completion, natural wipe, measured breathing reset, environmental micro-response and resumed exit—not the source woman, sheet, bicycle, bench, ferns, gate, clothing, exact camera, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Lock one clearly adult original subject and complete one ordinary tactile task before rest.
- Purpose: Gives stillness a before-state and purpose.
- Failure if removed: Opening directly on a bench becomes a generic portrait.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one natural foreground or architectural edge to transition into a distinct rest geometry.
- Purpose: Connects active and quiet states without arbitrary cutting.
- Failure if removed: A random dissolve loses spatial intention.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show exactly one slow breath and at least two micro-responses in body or environment.
- Purpose: Makes internal reset visible without melodrama.
- Failure if removed: No micro-response looks frozen; many gestures overplay the moment.

### inv-04

- Rule: Restrict camera movement during the breath to one slow push or hold.
- Purpose: Protects attention and temporal contrast.
- Failure if removed: Fast coverage destroys the reset.

### inv-05

- Rule: After rest, resume the same adult's movement and cross a visible exit threshold.
- Purpose: Proves renewal rather than terminal stillness.
- Failure if removed: Ending on the bench leaves no change in agency.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Lock one clearly adult original subject and complete one ordinary tactile task before rest.
- Use one natural foreground or architectural edge to transition into a distinct rest geometry.
- Restrict camera movement during the breath to one slow push or hold.
- After rest, resume the same adult's movement and cross a visible exit threshold.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- source woman, face, hair, outfit and exact expressions
- source white sheet, clips and outdoor task
- source stairs, bicycle foreground wipe, bench and ferns
- source park, brick gate, filtered light and exact route
- source MiniMax H3 same-post wording, camera, timing and creator identity

   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
