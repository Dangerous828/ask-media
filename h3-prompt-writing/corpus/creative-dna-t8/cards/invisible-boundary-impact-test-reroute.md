# 不可见边界验真｜碰撞、手测与改道离开

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one clearly adult original traveler, a simple route and a stable axis that visibly predicts free passage.
- Use exactly one uninterrupted unseen contact and preserve approach, contact and bodily recoil.
- Return to the same location for one controlled palm test and localize the boundary response around contact.
- End on a clear rerouting decision while the confirmed boundary stays fixed in the original path.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 不可见边界验真｜碰撞、手测与改道离开 — 摘要

## 用途

先用一条视觉上完全畅通的路线建立通行预期，再让明确成年角色在同一轴线上遭遇一次不可见阻断；必须完整显示接近、接触和身体回弹。角色先检查受影响部位，再回到碰撞点以手掌进行一次受控测试，让边界通过触感与涟漪证据显形；结尾角色明确改道离开，边界仍留在原路径。

## 推荐输入格式

`明确成年原创角色、看似可通行路线与稳定轴线先建立 + 只发生一次不可见接触且连续显示接近、接触与回弹 + 角色先检查受影响部位 + 回到准确接触点做一次受控手掌复测 + 只在接触周围用冷凝、粒子或涟漪局部显出透明平面 + 证据触发明确改道且边界保持固定`

# 不可见边界验真｜碰撞、手测与改道离开 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult traveler, one simple route and a stable camera axis that makes the space ahead look fully passable. Let the adult approach continuously until exactly one unseen boundary contact produces a readable full-body stop or recoil; preserve the approach, contact and consequence without a cut across the causal moment. Show one brief self-check on the affected body area, then return to the exact contact location for one controlled palm test. Reveal the boundary only through localized physical evidence such as pressure, condensation, particles or ripples while keeping the broader surface visually transparent. End with the adult making a clear rerouting decision and leaving the blocked axis while the boundary remains spatially stable. The reusable mechanism is false passability, one impact, bodily evidence, deliberate retest, localized invisible-plane proof and route correction—not the source character, face, hair, costume, corridor, magic accessory, ripple design, exact gestures, composition, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one clearly adult original traveler, a simple route and a stable axis that visibly predicts free passage.
- Purpose: Creates the expectation that the invisible boundary can later contradict.
- Failure if removed: If the route already looks blocked, impact reveals nothing.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use exactly one uninterrupted unseen contact and preserve approach, contact and bodily recoil.
- Purpose: Makes the hidden plane physically credible before visual effects explain it.
- Failure if removed: Cutting across contact or repeating impacts weakens the causal proof.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show one brief self-check that identifies the affected body area before any deliberate test.
- Purpose: Separates accidental impact from later investigation.
- Failure if removed: Immediate magic visualization would skip human diagnosis.

### inv-04

- Rule: Return to the same location for one controlled palm test and localize the boundary response around contact.
- Purpose: Turns inference into repeatable spatial evidence without making the whole wall opaque.
- Failure if removed: A global effect cannot prove where or how the surface was touched.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a clear rerouting decision while the confirmed boundary stays fixed in the original path.
- Purpose: Converts discovery into behavioral consequence and closes the route logic.
- Failure if removed: Walking through or forgetting the plane erases the evidence chain.
