# 阻断改道｜资源检查后落入替代活动

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one intended action and its enabling environmental condition before the blocker appears.
- Keep one external blocker visibly active while the character evaluates it.
- Confirm resource absence before beginning a setting-compatible alternative activity.
- End on stable emotional or behavioral evidence of acceptance while the blocker remains unresolved.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 阻断改道｜资源检查后落入替代活动 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one ordinary intended action and the environmental condition required to complete it. Introduce one visible external blocker, then show the character checking the specific enabling resource that could overcome it. Confirm that the resource is unavailable through legible physical evidence. Let the character choose a setting-compatible alternative activity, preserve the blocker in the background, and end on a stable affective proof that the revised plan is genuinely accepted. The reusable mechanism is intended action, external blocker, resource check, confirmed deficit, chosen alternative and settled payoff—not the source woman, bookstore, rain, umbrella, coffee, book, clothing, window layout, exact performance, creator identity or model claim.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one intended action and its enabling environmental condition before the blocker appears.
- Purpose: Gives the interruption a measurable goal cost.
- Failure if removed: Without a prior intention, the obstacle is only atmosphere.

### inv-02

- Rule: Keep one external blocker visibly active while the character evaluates it.
- Purpose: Maintains causal pressure across the decision.
- Failure if removed: Removing the blocker before the choice makes the pivot unmotivated.

### inv-03

- Rule: Show a physical check for one specific resource that could overcome the blocker.
- Purpose: Turns decision logic into observable action.
- Failure if removed: A purely facial reaction hides the causal test.

### inv-04

- Rule: Confirm resource absence before beginning a setting-compatible alternative activity.
- Purpose: Separates failed solution from deliberate replanning.
- Failure if removed: Starting the alternative too early makes it look preplanned.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on stable emotional or behavioral evidence of acceptance while the blocker remains unresolved.
- Purpose: Proves adaptation rather than temporary distraction.
- Failure if removed: Reopening the original goal prevents closure.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |
