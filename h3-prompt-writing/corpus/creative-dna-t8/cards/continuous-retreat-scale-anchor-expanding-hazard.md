# 连续拉远尺度揭危｜小型载体、扩张暗区与临界停留

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one small moving carrier with heading, speed cue and trail before the anomaly is legible.
- Use one uninterrupted camera retreat and elevation change with no reveal cut.
- Grow the anomaly from ambiguous texture into a coherent hazard occupying more of the frame.
- Hold before impact or disappearance with the carrier still readable.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 连续拉远尺度揭危｜小型载体、扩张暗区与临界停留 — 摘要

## 用途

先用一个持续前进的小型载体作为尺度锚点，镜头不切地逐步升高并后退；一个起初像普通暗纹的环境异常必须保持中心关系并不断占据更大画幅，载体的航迹、速度和尺寸持续可读，最终在进入不可逆临界区之前停留，让尺度和危险都由同一连续镜头证明。

## 推荐输入格式

`小型移动载体先建立方向速度与轨迹 + 摄影机全程不切地持续升高后退 + 载体与异常保持同一坐标关系 + 异常从模糊纹理成长为占据主要画幅的清楚危险 + 撞击或消失前停留且载体仍可读`

# 连续拉远尺度揭危｜小型载体、扩张暗区与临界停留 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original small moving carrier with a readable heading, speed cue and trail before an environmental anomaly is legible. Use one continuous camera retreat and elevation change with no reveal cut, keeping the carrier and anomaly in the same coordinate relationship as more environment enters the frame. Let the anomaly evolve from an ambiguous dark or geometric texture into a coherent hazard that occupies progressively more of the composition, while the carrier remains the stable scale unit. End on a held pre-impact or threshold state before the carrier disappears. The reusable mechanism is moving scale anchor, uninterrupted context expansion, ambiguity-to-hazard reveal and critical-distance hold—not the source wooden ship, sail, ocean, whirlpool, Odyssey claim, exact camera path, water physics, palette, watermark, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one small moving carrier with heading, speed cue and trail before the anomaly is legible.
- Purpose: Provides a stable comparison unit for every later scale reveal.
- Failure if removed: Without a moving anchor, the hazard's size and distance remain abstract.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use one uninterrupted camera retreat and elevation change with no reveal cut.
- Purpose: Lets scale emerge from added context rather than editorial substitution.
- Failure if removed: A cut can fake scale and break the promised continuous observation.

### inv-03

- Rule: Keep carrier and anomaly in one coordinate relationship as the frame expands.
- Purpose: Makes approach distance and danger continuously measurable.
- Failure if removed: Reframing them independently removes spatial causality.

### inv-04

- Rule: Grow the anomaly from ambiguous texture into a coherent hazard occupying more of the frame.
- Purpose: Turns curiosity into threat through visual evidence alone.
- Failure if removed: Showing the full hazard immediately removes the escalation.

### inv-05

- Rule: Hold before impact or disappearance with the carrier still readable.
- Purpose: Preserves the final scale comparison and avoids replacing tension with destruction.
- Failure if removed: Losing the carrier prevents verification of the critical distance.
