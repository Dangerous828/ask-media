# 炫技预期反转｜高调费力铺垫、安静难动作与社交重排

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish two clearly adult original performers and at least two adult witnesses in one readable social space.
- Make the first performer invest in visible preparation, effort and attention seeking.
- Show witness eye lines and positions move from the first performer to the rival.
- End with both adults and witnesses held in the reordered arrangement.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 炫技预期反转｜高调费力铺垫、安静难动作与社交重排 — 摘要

## 用途

先让明确成年炫技者通过准备仪式、巨大努力与主动索取目光建立社会预期；安静对手不争抢镜头，只完整完成一次更难且更干净的动作。围观者的眼线、站位与表情随后从前者转向后者，终局用双方克制反应证明地位重排。

## 推荐输入格式

`两名明确成年原创表演者和至少两名成年见证者在同一可读社交空间 + 第一人用准备仪式、明显费力与索取注意建立强预期 + 安静对手不抢镜并在连续镜头中完成一次更难或更精确动作 + 见证者眼线和站位从第一人转向对手 + 终局两名成年人和见证者共同保持重新排序后的关系`

# 炫技预期反转｜高调费力铺垫、安静难动作与社交重排 Template

## Mechanism

Establish one original clearly adult showy performer, one clearly adult quiet rival and at least two adult witnesses in the same social geography. Let the first performer use visible preparation, effort and attention-seeking behavior to create a strong expectation. Introduce the rival without fanfare and prove competence through one continuous materially harder or more precise action, with no hidden cuts. Then map the social reversal through witness eye lines, positions and restrained reactions before holding both performers together. The reusable mechanism is expectation investment, quiet competence proof, audience-attention transfer and status reordering—not the source bodies, gym, exercises, glasses, woman, comedy gestures, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult original performers and at least two adult witnesses in one readable social space.
- Purpose: Makes status a group relation rather than private comparison.
- Failure if removed: Without witnesses, attention cannot visibly transfer.

### inv-02

- Rule: Make the first performer invest in visible preparation, effort and attention seeking.
- Purpose: Builds a clear expectation to reverse.
- Failure if removed: A brief ordinary action gives the reversal no weight.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let the quiet rival complete one harder or more precise action continuously without fanfare.
- Purpose: Provides objective competence proof.
- Failure if removed: Hidden cuts or cheering before completion weaken credibility.

### inv-04

- Rule: Show witness eye lines and positions move from the first performer to the rival.
- Purpose: Externalizes the social status change.
- Failure if removed: Reaction close-ups without spatial mapping are ambiguous.

### inv-05

- Rule: End with both adults and witnesses held in the reordered arrangement.
- Purpose: Confirms durable reversal.
- Failure if removed: Cutting immediately after the skill becomes only a stunt highlight.
