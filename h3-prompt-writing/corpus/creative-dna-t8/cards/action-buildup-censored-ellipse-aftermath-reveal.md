# 刻意遮断省略｜动作升级后以审查卡跳到余波结果

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish two clearly adult-scale original figures and stable geography before escalation.
- Show at least three complete visible action beats before the interruption.
- Never depict, narrate or infer hidden action during the interruption.
- Return to the same geography and reveal only observable aftermath states.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

## 用途

先用明确成年角色和原创对手完成可读动作升级；到最大冲突前主动切入可辨识的原创遮断卡，让缺失成为叙事省略而不是伪造动作。遮断期间只保留克制声画提示，随后回到同一空间、同一人物与可验证余波，不推断被遮内容。

## 推荐输入格式

`两名明确成年原创对立主体和稳定地理先建立 + 至少三次完整可见动作逐级升级 + 最大阈值切入可辨识的原创遮断卡而非故障画面 + 遮断期间绝不展示、描述或推断隐藏动作 + 回到同一地理只呈现可观察余波并保持结果不解释`

# 刻意遮断省略｜动作升级后以审查卡跳到余波结果 Template

## Mechanism

Establish two original clearly adult-scale opposing figures and one stable confrontation geography. Escalate through at least three fully visible action beats, then at the maximum threshold deliberately replace the picture with an unmistakably authored original interruption card or graphic blackout. Do not depict, describe or infer the missing action. During the ellipse, use only restrained temporal evidence such as distant sound, rain or a counter. Return to the same geography and reveal only observable aftermath states of the same figures and environment. The reusable mechanism is legible buildup, explicit authored censorship-as-ellipsis, non-invention and aftermath reveal—not the source fighters, armor, alley, rain palette, CENSORED word, choreography, hidden content, creator identity or timing.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish two clearly adult-scale original figures and stable geography before escalation.
- Purpose: Makes pre/post comparison possible.
- Failure if removed: An undefined space prevents aftermath verification.

### inv-02

- Rule: Show at least three complete visible action beats before the interruption.
- Purpose: Earns the narrative threshold.
- Failure if removed: An early blackout feels like missing media rather than intentional form.

### inv-03

- Rule: Use an unmistakably authored original interruption card at the maximum threshold.
- Purpose: Labels absence as deliberate ellipsis.
- Failure if removed: A corrupt-looking cut can be mistaken for decode failure.

### inv-04

- Rule: Never depict, narrate or infer hidden action during the interruption.
- Purpose: Preserves evidence honesty and audience imagination.
- Failure if removed: Invented combat falsely claims source support.

### inv-05

- Rule: Return to the same geography and reveal only observable aftermath states.
- Purpose: Closes the comparison without solving the missing interval.
- Failure if removed: A new location or explanatory flashback breaks the ellipse.
