# 群体随拍｜自拍锚点、快切活动到共同收束

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Read [references/summary.md](references/summary.md) for fit, input slots, and the concise starting example.
2. Read [references/template.md](references/template.md) completely before drafting.
3. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
4. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Keep one clearly adult original selfie carrier recognizable across every phase.
- Stay within one coherent gathering and repeat at least two environmental anchors.
- Increase the readable group state through at least three stages while retaining natural imperfect phone behavior.
- End on the largest group state and one complete shared action.

5. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
6. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
7. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 群体随拍｜自拍锚点、快切活动到共同收束 Template

## Mechanism

Use one clearly adult original selfie carrier as the identity anchor across a rapid native-phone montage in one coherent gathering. Alternate short activity inserts with repeated returns to the carrier, and let the visible group grow in countable stages: setup, task, arrivals, peak collective reaction and complete shared gesture. Preserve recurring environmental anchors, ordinary jump cuts, slight shake, focus breathing and late recentering without losing the carrier. End on the largest readable group state and one completed communal action. The reusable mechanism is selfie anchor, activity proof, progressive group growth, imperfect phone grammar and terminal communal gesture—not the source woman, face, sports clothing, stadium, parking lot, car trunks, grills, burgers, drinks, team gear, friends, exact prompt, timing, creator identity or model label.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Keep one clearly adult original selfie carrier recognizable across every phase.
- Purpose: Provides identity continuity through rapid cuts.
- Failure if removed: Changing the carrier turns the sequence into an unrelated compilation.

### inv-02

- Rule: Stay within one coherent gathering and repeat at least two environmental anchors.
- Purpose: Makes jump cuts read as one event.
- Failure if removed: Unrelated locations destroy memory continuity.

### inv-03

- Rule: Alternate activity proof with returns to the carrier rather than showing only faces.
- Purpose: Answers what the group is doing and why it grows.
- Failure if removed: A face-only montage lacks causal event progression.

### inv-04

- Rule: Increase the readable group state through at least three stages while retaining natural imperfect phone behavior.
- Purpose: Builds social escalation without staged coverage.
- Failure if removed: A constant group or polished stabilization flattens the candid arc.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on the largest group state and one complete shared action.
- Purpose: Provides a countable terminal memory payoff.
- Failure if removed: Ending mid-pan or before the gesture leaves the event unresolved.


## Variable Slots

| Slot | Constraint |
|---|---|
| lead_subject | An original stable subject or pair with a readable role. |
| setting | A new setting with fixed spatial anchors and no source-specific surface. |
| escalation_variable | One causal variable that changes through visible stages. |
| performance_rule | A restrained acting or motion rule that keeps the mechanism legible. |
| camera_grammar | An information order that proves each state without copying source shot boundaries. |
| payoff | A clean final physical, informational or social consequence. |
| sound_family | An original hierarchy of diegetic cues; no unsupported source-audio claim. |

## Required Human-Facing Anchors

- Keep one clearly adult original selfie carrier recognizable across every phase.
- Stay within one coherent gathering and repeat at least two environmental anchors.
- Increase the readable group state through at least three stages while retaining natural imperfect phone behavior.
- End on the largest group state and one complete shared action.

## Anti-Copy Boundary

- the source woman's likeness, hair, clothing, smile, gestures and exact selfie angles
- the source stadium parking lot, car trunks, coolers, grills, burgers, drinks and sports gear
- the source friends, team identities, group arrangement, cheers, high-fives and toast timing
- the source rapid-cut order, camera shake pattern, focus changes, exposure and color treatment
- the source creator identity, exact prompt wording, Seedance 2.0 claim, platform overlay, watermark and branding

   - Changed: lead_subject, setting, escalation_variable, performance_rule, camera_grammar, payoff, sound_family
