# 画内涂鸦｜现实互动逐步绘出隐藏世界

Create a new execution from the reusable mechanism, not a surface reskin of any reference video.

## Workflow

1. Preserve the user's subject and objective, then fill every missing scene, trigger, event-chain, camera, sound, and payoff slot.
2. Treat these anchors as mandatory:

- Establish one embodied observer, one responsive subject and the first surface-bound drawing triggered by visible interaction.
- Use a second real action to extend the drawing rule onto a different local surface or route.
- Reveal one new local figure or motif that visibly inherits an earlier line family.
- End on a wider view where prior symbols connect into one coherent environment-wide drawing layer.

3. Change source-specific people, setting, props, palette, dialogue, shot boundaries, sound family, and payoff surfaces together.
4. Compile MiniMax H3 and Seedance 2.0 independently; do not leak one model's field syntax into the other.
5. Silently verify anchor coverage, continuity, timing, physical causality, and anti-copy distance before returning the final prompts.

## MiniMax H3

- Use the current official H3 task grammar and language profile.
- Separate integrated visual action, overall soundscape, and non-diegetic music or explicit silence.
- Use timestamps only to make event order executable; keep shots consecutive and duration-safe.

## Seedance 2.0

- Define stable subjects once and preserve event order, camera motivation, sound intent, and overall duration.
- Use a simple paragraph for simple concepts or an ordered multi-shot structure for complex concepts.
- State overall duration once; do not copy H3 field labels or per-shot timecode grammar.

## Output

Return one concept sentence, the filled slots, an anchor-coverage check, one MiniMax H3 prompt, one Seedance 2.0 prompt, and a short anti-copy validation note.

## Non-Negotiable

- A subject-only request is underspecified. Preserve that subject and complete the mechanism instead of returning a generic portrait or showcase.
- Do not omit, reorder, or weaken a required anchor.
- Do not reuse source people, brands, dialogue, exact storyboard, signature props, exact timings, or recognizable effect surfaces.

# 画内涂鸦｜现实互动逐步绘出隐藏世界 Template

## Mechanism

Use an embodied observer and one responsive subject to author a diegetic drawing layer through visible real-world actions. Each interaction must add one new surface-anchored symbol that echoes the local object or motion, and later symbols must reuse or connect earlier marks. Escalate from single icon to route, hidden figure and local reflection, then end wide enough to prove the accumulated drawings have become one coherent environment-wide system.

## Invariants

### inv-01

- Rule: Establish one embodied observer, one responsive subject and the first surface-bound drawing triggered by visible interaction.
- Purpose: Defines who authors the graphic layer and where it belongs.
- Failure if removed: Unmotivated floating stickers make the effect decorative.

### inv-02

- Rule: Use a second real action to extend the drawing rule onto a different local surface or route.
- Purpose: Proves that the graphic response follows physical behavior.
- Failure if removed: Repeating the same icon over the same hand never builds a world.

### inv-03

- Rule: Let accumulated drawings direct attention toward one hidden or newly opened area.
- Purpose: Turns symbols into spatial storytelling.
- Failure if removed: Random icons with no search function cannot hand off to discovery.

### inv-04

- Rule: Reveal one new local figure or motif that visibly inherits an earlier line family.
- Purpose: Creates graphic continuity across separate interactions.
- Failure if removed: A completely unrelated illustration breaks the accumulated system.

### inv-05

- Rule: End on a wider view where prior symbols connect into one coherent environment-wide drawing layer.
- Purpose: Pays off local marks as a transformed world.
- Failure if removed: Ending on a single sticker or close-up leaves the escalation incomplete.
