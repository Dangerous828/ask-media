# 不可能奢华姿态｜极远尺度钩子、连续推进与配饰细节阶梯

## 用途

以不可能但静稳的成年时装姿态作为远景钩子，持续推进依次验证脸、手中动作、主配饰与鞋履细节；环境和姿态保持连续，最终以材质特写完成奢华证明。

## 推荐输入格式

`Establish one clearly adult original luxury subject in an impossible but readable pose against measurable vast scale. + Use one continuous approach or coherent lens ladder to verify the same body, outfit and environment. + Allow one restrained hand action to create a small controlled accent without disturbing the pose. + Give face and one primary accessory separate readable material-proof intervals. + Finish on a lower-body or product macro that retains the same light and environment rather than cutting to an unrelated packshot.`

## 可直接改写的简短示例

> 玫瑰色黎明原创镜面盐原上空极远景：一名明确成年中东腕表设计师穿炭灰长西装，像失重般坐着，持细黄铜资料夹并佩戴一枚可见原创钢表。；她只转动一次表冠；一圈微小晨光沿表圈运行，一滴悬浮盐水升起后落稳。；以炭灰靴和它悬在盐镜上方的倒影收尾，资料夹边缘仍可见；同一环境停住，不加品牌文字。

## 必须保留的结构锚点

- Establish one clearly adult original luxury subject in an impossible but readable pose against measurable vast scale.
- Use one continuous approach or coherent lens ladder to verify the same body, outfit and environment.
- Give face and one primary accessory separate readable material-proof intervals.
- Finish on a lower-body or product macro that retains the same light and environment rather than cutting to an unrelated packshot.

## 使用方法

1. 保留用户给出的主体与目标。
2. 按推荐输入格式补齐场景、事件链、镜头职责和结果。
3. 同时改变表面名词、风格、光影、声效与物理实现，不做来源视频换名词复刻。
4. 分别编译 MiniMax H3 与 Seedance 2.0，并逐项核对结构锚点。
